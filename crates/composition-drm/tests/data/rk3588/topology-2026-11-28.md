## Findings: probe's derived view vs kernel state

1. **Format truncation.** probe prints at most 32 formats per plane. The Cluster planes have more than 32 (format, modifier) pairs, so the probe output cannot show the complete IN_FORMATS list or whether the Cluster planes also list `DRM_FORMAT_MOD_LINEAR`. The debugfs state shows the fbcon buffer scanning out of `Cluster0-win0` with modifier 0x0, so linear is accepted there. Complete modifier data needs the raw property dump (props example, not landed in this snapshot).
2. **Only connected outputs are modelled.** probe lists one output (`video_port0`), while the kernel exposes two CRTCs. Plane masks are expressed over that single output, so Cluster1 (plane 39) shows `outputs 0b0` although it is a Primary-type plane. Its real `possible_crtcs` was not read. This matters for plane assignment on a dual-output setup.
3. **Plane types.** Cluster0 and Cluster1 are reported Primary (one per video port in VOP2); the remaining six are Overlay. A plane-to-CRTC planner that assumes one primary per output has Cluster1 unusable until HDMI-A-2 or a second connector is active.
4. **zpos.** The probe reports 0..=7 for all eight planes. The kernel's normalized-zpos was 0..7, one per plane, at reset. The kernel property range was not read directly.
5. **Scaling and rotation** are not reported by probe ("scaling not claimed"); the debugfs dump shows `rotation=1` only. Real scaling limits and the rotation bitmask need the props example or the source.
6. **Alpha and blend.** probe claims plane alpha and three blend modes on all planes (derived from `alpha` and `pixel blend mode` properties). Not cross-checked against a raw dump here; `vop2/summary` shows `glb_alpha[0xff]` on the active plane, which agrees that the plane alpha state exists.
