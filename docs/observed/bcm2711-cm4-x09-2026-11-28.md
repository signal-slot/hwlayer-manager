# BCM2711 (reTerminal CM4): X-09 re-probe (plane enumeration on vc4)

Re-run of the `probe` example on the same board as `docs/observed/bcm2711-cm4.md`, to close X-09 in
`docs/open-issues.md` after commit 92a74e0 ("drm: keep planes that reach a probed output before
truncating"). Results tagged **observed 2026-11-28** were measured over ssh on 2026-11-28 (host UTC
01:04:19; the board's clock agreed). Portability evidence only; it counts toward no milestone.
`profile_check` was not used.

## Before / after (output 0 = CRTC 90, the only connected CRTC)

| Item | Before (112648f / b0f3ef3) | After (d48b9b0) | Tag |
|---|---|---|---|
| Planes reported | 16 | 16 (the `MAX_PLANES` cap) | observed 2026-11-28 |
| Primaries of unconnected CRTCs (mask `0b0`) taking slots | 5 (48, 67, 91, 103, 115) | 0 | observed 2026-11-28 |
| Primary of output 0 | 79 | 79 | observed 2026-11-28 |
| Overlays reaching output 0 | 10 (127 … 226) | 14 (127 … 270) | observed 2026-11-28 |
| Cursor of output 0 | none | 677 | observed 2026-11-28 |
| Planes with mask `0b1` | 11 | 16 (all of them) | observed 2026-11-28 |

The kernel has 16 shared overlays reaching CRTC 90 plus its cursor (`bcm2711-cm4.md`, "Topology").
With `MAX_PLANES = 16` the list holds primary + 14 overlays + cursor, so two overlays are still cut.
That is the cap, not the X-09 ordering fault. Raising `MAX_PLANES` is not part of this item.

## Verdict

X-09 re-probe: PASS

Output 0 keeps its primary, its cursor and 14 overlays, and no primary of an unconnected CRTC takes a
slot. Caveat: 14 of 16 overlays, because of the cap (above).
